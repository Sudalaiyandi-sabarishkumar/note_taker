Priya Nair: Morning everyone. Let's scope the HomeFix booking platform. Start with how a customer books a job.
Jose Ruiz: A customer picks a service category, describes the problem, and picks a time window. The booking is confirmed only once a provider accepts it.
Priya Nair: How is a provider chosen?
Jose Ruiz: The system auto-assigns the nearest available provider who has a rating of at least four stars. If no provider accepts within 60 minutes, the job goes to a manual dispatch queue.
Priya Nair: Good. What does the customer see while they wait?
Jose Ruiz: A live status screen showing the assigned provider's name, photo, and estimated arrival time, once the job is accepted.
Priya Nair: Accessibility - anything specific there?
Jose Ruiz: The booking screens must meet WCAG 2.1 AA, and the whole flow has to work in a mobile browser without a native app.
Priya Nair: Provider onboarding?
Jose Ruiz: A new provider signs up, uploads their trade qualifications, and is activated only after an admin approves them. Providers cannot approve themselves.
Priya Nair: On notifications - what gets sent to the customer?
Jose Ruiz: The customer gets an email when the booking is confirmed and another when the provider is on the way.
Priya Nair: Anything else on notifications?
Jose Ruiz: Yes, also send the customer an SMS when the provider is fifteen minutes away.
Priya Nair: Let's talk money.
Jose Ruiz: Customers pay by card or cash on completion, but an itemised invoice is emailed within 24 hours of the job closing.
Priya Nair: Is there a minimum charge?
Jose Ruiz: There is a 45 dollar call-out fee, and it applies even if the customer cancels after the provider has already been dispatched.
Priya Nair: Cancellations in general?
Jose Ruiz: A customer can cancel free of charge up to two hours before the window starts. Within two hours, the call-out fee is charged. Three cancellations in a rolling 30 days and the account is flagged for review.
Priya Nair: Ratings?
Jose Ruiz: After the job the customer rates the provider from one to five stars and can leave an optional written review. The provider's average rating is shown publicly on their profile.
Priya Nair: Is there a quick way to re-book the same provider?
Jose Ruiz: There is a re-book button that repeats a customer's last job in one tap.
Priya Nair: What about refunds if the customer is unhappy with the work?
