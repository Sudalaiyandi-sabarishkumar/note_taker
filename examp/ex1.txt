Attachments Amazon Curated Storefront — Discovery Call 1 Notes by Gemini 

Meeting records Transcript 

Summary 

Team finalised the scope for a two-part commerce platform covering a customer storefront and an internal administration application. 

Platform structure agreed 

The engagement will deliver a responsive customer website alongside an administrative application for the category operations team. Customer authentication will be passwordless using mobile number verification, while administrators will authenticate with email and password credentials. 

Catalogue and inventory management 

Stock will be managed within the admin application, with the category operations team of 50 users maintaining product listings and order records. The initial launch targets the India market with rupee pricing. 

Timeline and deferred items 

A demonstrable version is required within approximately ten weeks for a leadership review. Payment processing and single sign-on integration were deferred to later phases, and wishlist functionality was explicitly excluded. 

Decisions 

Aligned 

Two-part platform scope confirmed The engagement covers a customer-facing responsive website for product browsing and purchase, plus an internal admin application for managing product listings, stock, and orders. 

Authentication approach settled Customers will sign up and log in using mobile number with OTP verification and no password. Administrators will use email and password. 

India-first launch The initial release targets the India market only, with pricing in rupees. 

Shelved 

SSO integration deferred Internal single sign-on for the admin application is out of scope for phase one and may be revisited later. 

Wishlist functionality excluded The stakeholder confirmed wishlists should not be built despite anticipated user requests.
